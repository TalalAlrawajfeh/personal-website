This work studies how to account for privacy noise in differentially private variational inference, particularly for high dimensional, nonconjugate models. The research combines a mathematical formulation of noise aware inference with empirical evaluation on Bayesian regression tasks.

Published in the *Proceedings of the 28th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Statistics* (AISTATS 2025).
