The paper identifies the output label space of a classifier as a potential privacy side channel when labels depend on sensitive data. It formalizes privacy for continual learning and evaluates mechanisms for releasing labels while preserving valid differential privacy guarantees.

Marlon Tobaben and Talal Alrawajfeh contributed equally.
